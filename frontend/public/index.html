How it works

Three steps. All of them automatic.

Every ticket sale runs through the same settlement path — a real ledger entry, not a promise to pay you later.

01

Fan pays via Crypto or Nomba

Checkout accepts cards, bank transfers, or USDT/BTC/ETH. Payment clears instantly with zero OTP bottlenecks.

02

Ledger settles the split

Your cut and the platform fee are calculated and recorded the moment payment confirms — split percentages are fully dynamic.

03

Payout hits your bank

Naira lands in your account same day. No 30-day hold, no minimum payout threshold, no support ticket to chase it.
